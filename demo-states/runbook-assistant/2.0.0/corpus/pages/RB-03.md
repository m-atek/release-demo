Every release and rollback is a change in ServiceNow, opened by the pipeline as release.pipeline, with the evidence attached. Alex Change Manager, in Change approvers, approves a normal change in ServiceNow, and the pipeline waits up to 30 minutes for that approval.

## Normal changes

A normal change moves to Assess, where ServiceNow asks the group Change approvers, which is Alex Change Manager. The pipeline polls the change every 15 seconds for up to 30 minutes. Alex's approval starts production within 20 seconds, and Slack says "Approved by Alex in ServiceNow." A rejection stops the run and cancels the change; so does a time-out.

## Standard changes

A routine release with `standard`, a rollback, and every release of a service whose services.yaml entry says so, come from a pre-approved standard change template, such as "Routine release of orders-service" or "Rollback of orders-service". They skip the Approval stage.

## What the change holds

The CMDB item, who asked, the Jira issue, the pipeline's stages as the implementation plan, the automatic rollback as the backout plan, the test results, a work note per stage, and the JUnit report, SBOM, scan report and build-info.json as attachments.
