pricing-api's releases pass two checks the other services don't have, and need no approval: each is a pre-approved standard change.

## The contract check

Right after SBOM and scan, oasdiff compares the package's openapi.yaml with the one in the release production runs, as JFrog keeps it. A breaking change, such as a removed response field, stops the run before anything is published, and Slack names what broke, for example "Contract check failed: field currency removed".

## The canary

In production the new version gets 10% of the traffic for 5 minutes (3 minutes with the option `call`), while two CloudWatch alarms watch that version alone: errors at or above 3 a minute, and a p95 duration above 800 ms. On an alarm its share goes back to 0% within seconds and the change closes as unsuccessful. Without one it takes all the traffic.
