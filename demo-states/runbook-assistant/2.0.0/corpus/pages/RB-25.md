A change to the network stack made outside Terraform, such as a security group edited in the AWS console, is found by itself: the drift check starts within a minute or two, Slack reports the drift, and ServiceNow gets an incident.

## How it's found

CloudTrail hands the console call to EventBridge, and the drift trigger starts the job network-drift, usually within a minute or two. The job also runs at 07:00 on weekdays. It plans the stack against main.

## What you get

Slack posts ":warning: Drift in network-stack: AWS no longer matches main.", names what changed and how many seconds after the console change it was found, and ServiceNow gets an incident. The check reports drift; it doesn't revert it. Apply main again through a pull request, or let the next apply put it back.
