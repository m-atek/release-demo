A release builds its package once and deploys exactly that package everywhere.

## Candidates and releases

Publish uploads the package and build-info.json, with their checksums, to the candidates repository: `<service>-candidates/<version>/<build>/`, or `demos-candidates/<service>/` for the newer services. Record copies the package that reached production to the releases repository.

## Deployed by checksum

Staging and production each download the package from JFrog again and verify its SHA-256 against the one recorded at build. A package changed after publishing fails its checksum, and the deploy stops. The pipeline's JFrog user may upload but not delete, so nothing published can be overwritten.
