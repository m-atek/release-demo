Production's check runs right after the production deploy: /health must report the new version, and the service's check paths must answer, within the time services.yaml sets (60 seconds for orders-service).

## When it fails

The pipeline rolls back on its own: production goes back to the previous version, which is checked too. The change closes as unsuccessful with the rollback and the recovery time in its notes, Jira gets a comment, Grafana gets a rollback marker, and Slack says "Production check failed. Rolled back to" the previous version.

## The usual cause

A setting production doesn't have. orders-service 1.4.1 read the delivery estimate under a new name that staging had and production didn't, so it passed staging and failed in production.
