Rotate the JFrog token every quarter, or at once if it leaks.

## Steps

Sign in to JFrog as admin, create a new token for the pipeline user, and paste it into the Jenkins credential. Revoke the old token in JFrog. Tell the team in #releases that releases may fail for a few minutes.
