The Releases dashboard in Grafana shows four delivery metrics for the last 14 days, computed from the production events every release sends.

## The four

Deployment frequency, lead time for changes (from the commit to the production deploy), change failure rate, and failed deployment recovery time. The dashboard also shows the version in staging and production, the last ten releases with their change numbers and results, and Lambda errors and duration with every release and rollback marked.
