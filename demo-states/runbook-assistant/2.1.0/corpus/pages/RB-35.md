Jenkins, JFrog and Splunk are published through a Cloudflare tunnel that the tools host dials out to, at jenkins-demo.es.nl, artifactory-demo.es.nl and splunk-demo.es.nl.

## Who gets in

Each has a Cloudflare Access application: people sign in with their allowed email address, and machines, such as the Slack handler and the build agents outside AWS, send their own service token. Each token can be revoked on its own. Without Access, the tools show nothing.
