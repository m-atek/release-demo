The release pipeline has twelve stages, the same for every service, plus a few a service adds where it needs them.

## The twelve

Build, Test, Quality gate, SBOM and scan, Publish, Staging, Check staging, Change, Approval, Production, Check production and Record. Each stage updates the one Slack message, adds a work note to the change once it exists, and sends one event to Splunk and Grafana.

## Stages a service adds

pricing-api adds a Contract check after SBOM and scan. store-sync adds a Plan stage before the change, for its waves. runbook-assistant adds an Evaluation after Check staging.

## When it stops

A failed test, quality gate or critical finding stops the run before staging, and Slack says "Stopped after" the total time and "Nothing reached production." Any change that was open is cancelled.
