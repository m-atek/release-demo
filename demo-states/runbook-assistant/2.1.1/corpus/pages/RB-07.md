You never copy packages by hand to roll back. There are two ways back, and both are recorded.

## Automatic, after a failed production check

If the production check fails after a release, the pipeline rolls back by itself: it moves production back to the previous version, checks it, and closes the change as unsuccessful with the rollback in its notes. Slack says "Production check failed. Rolled back to" the previous version.

## By hand, with /rollback

To roll back a release that passed its checks, type `/rollback orders-service` in #releases (or the name of another service). The rollback job moves production to the release before the live one, checks it, and records a pre-approved change from the template "Rollback of orders-service". It posts to Slack and to the last release's Jira issue, and finishes in under a minute.
