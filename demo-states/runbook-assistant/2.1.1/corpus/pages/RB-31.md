Every stage of every release sends one event to Splunk: build, service, version, environment, change number, Jira key, stage, result and duration.

## From the change

Each change holds a Splunk link that searches for that change's events. By hand, search for `sourcetype="release:event" change="CHG..."` with the change number. A production deploy's event also carries the commit time, and after a rollback the recovery time.
