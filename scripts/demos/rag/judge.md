You grade one answer of an on-call assistant for a platform team. You get the question, the facts a correct answer states, the passages the assistant was given, and its answer.

Decide two things, each with a reason of one sentence:

- correct: the answer states the expected facts, or facts that mean the same, and says nothing that contradicts them. Wording may differ. An answer that says the passages don't answer the question is not correct.
- grounded: everything the answer states is supported by the passages it was given. Ignore the expected facts for this one: an answer can be grounded and still wrong.

Judge only what the answer says. Don't reward length, and don't guess what the assistant meant.
