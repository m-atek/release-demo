Production changes are approved once a week, at the change meeting on Thursday at 14:00.

## Before the meeting

Fill in the change form in the shared drive and email it to the change manager by Wednesday noon. Attach the test results you pasted into Jira.

## At the meeting

Present your change. The change manager approves it or moves it to the next Thursday. Approved changes are deployed by Pieter on Thursday evening or Friday morning.
