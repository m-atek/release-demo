In #releases, type `/ask` and your question, between 3 and 300 characters. Anyone in the team may ask; you don't need to be a releaser.

## What you get

The Release Bot posts your question, then updates the same message with the answer, the pages it used as "Sources", each with its status, and the version of the assistant that answered. An answer usually lands within a minute. The assistant answers from production's runbooks and past changes only, and says so when they don't answer your question.

## What it won't take

A question that looks like it holds a secret, such as a key or a token, isn't sent anywhere: "Not asked: the question looked like it held a secret."
