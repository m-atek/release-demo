catalog is a container on Kubernetes, released with `/release catalog <Jira key>` under a pre-approved standard change.

## The deploy

Helm 4 runs `helm upgrade --install --rollback-on-failure --wait=watcher --timeout 3m`, with the image pinned by digest. If the new pods aren't ready within 3 minutes, Helm rolls back to the previous revision by itself; the old pods serve throughout. The change then closes as unsuccessful.

## Access

Jenkins deploys as a service account that may change only staging and production, with a token that lasts 10 minutes. No long-lived cluster token is stored.
