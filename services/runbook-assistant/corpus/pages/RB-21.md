catalog's production on the cluster host comes only from Git: deploy/production/catalog. Argo CD applies it, and puts back any change made to the cluster by hand.

## On call

Break-glass access is a client certificate for your own name, in the group release-demo:break-glass, signed for one hour. It may scale and patch catalog's deployment in production and nothing else. A hand edit is put back by Argo CD within seconds, and the platform posts it to Slack and opens an incident naming who made it, from the cluster's audit log.

## A fix that lasts

Open a pull request to deploy/production/catalog with the incident number in its title. The job catalog-production checks it, opens an emergency change linked to the incident, merges it after Change approvers approve, and waits for Argo CD to sync it. The incident is then resolved with that change.
