Store Floor, the app on the store phones and tablets, releases two ways.

## To the stores, through Jamf

`/release store-floor <Jira key> jamf` puts the new version on the pilot devices first, and on every store device after Alex approves the change.

## To testers

`/release store-floor <Jira key> stores` sends it to TestFlight and to Google Play's internal track, with the release notes from the Jira version, under a pre-approved standard change.

## Rolling back

`/rollback store-floor` rebuilds the previous release with a higher build number and gives it to Jamf, because iOS won't install an older build over a newer one.
