Normal changes are approved in ServiceNow by the group Change approvers, which is Alex Change Manager, while the pipeline waits up to 30 minutes for the approval. Nobody in it asks for releases.

## The person who asked

The person who asked for a release can never approve it. The pipeline refuses such an approval whatever ServiceNow says, and cancels the change. Sam, who asks for releases, is in Platform team and gets no approval request.

## Machines

release.pipeline opens and updates changes but approves nothing. Standard changes are pre-approved by their templates.
